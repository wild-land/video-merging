Bundled FFmpeg executable
==========================

The Linux FFmpeg executable in this application is extracted without changes
from the imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 platform wheel published on PyPI:
https://pypi.org/project/imageio-ffmpeg/0.6.0/

x86_64 wheel SHA-256:
c7e46fcec401dd990405049d2e2f475e2b397779df2519b544b8aab515195282

aarch64 wheel SHA-256:
1d47bebd83d2c5fc770720d211855f208af8a596c82d17730aa51e815cdee6dc

The bundled binaries report --enable-gpl and --enable-version3. FFmpeg is
licensed under the GNU GPL for these builds. The GPLv3 text is included in
GPL-3.0.txt. FFmpeg project source and licensing information:
https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

The imageio-ffmpeg Python wrapper is not bundled. Its BSD-2-Clause license
is included as imageio-ffmpeg-LICENSE because the executable is extracted
from the wheel distribution.
